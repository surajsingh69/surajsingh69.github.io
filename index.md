## What Makes a Tool "Free Enough"?

We test every tool against these criteria:

1. **No credit card required to start**
2. **Free tier is functional for real work** (not just a 3-use demo)
3. **No permanent watermark on free exports**
4. **Commercial use allowed on free tier** (where applicable)
5. **Tool is actively maintained in 2026**

If a tool fails any of these, we say so plainly.
